# Recipes

## Pasta e Ceci
Olive oil, garlic, chilli flakes, carrot, onion, salt, passata, chickpeas (blend 1/3), small pasta, veg broth. Season to taste. Serve with olive oil and veg hard cheese.

## Parathas
2 cups flour, ½ tsp salt, 1 tsp oil, ¾–1 cup water. Knead, rest 30 min. Roll out, spread oil and flour, fold letter-style, re-roll. Cook in hot pan 1–2 min/side with oil.

Stuffings:
- Aloo: mashed potato, onion, chilli, turmeric, cumin
- Gobi: grated cauliflower squeezed dry, onion, chilli, cumin
- Spiced dough: cumin/ajwain/chilli mixed in

## Carrot & Sweet Potato Rösti
Grate carrots and sweet potato, wring dry. Mix with cumin and chilli flakes. Shallow-fry in ghee until golden. Serve with poached eggs and spiced passata dip.

## Römertopf Coconut Chickpea & Butterbean Curry Bake
Chickpeas, butterbeans, veggie sausages, passata, coconut milk, miso paste. Bake in Römertopf.

## Minestrone
Bag of mixed veg. Half the recipe for 200g veg, full tin of beans.

## Bulgur Wheat
Toast dry 1–2 min. Add 1.5–2x hot stock, simmer covered 10–12 min, rest 5 min, fluff with fork.

## Posh Heinz Baked Beans
Passata, smoked paprika, miso, sugar, pinch of onion powder, pinch of garlic powder, chilli flakes, salt. Simmer 10 min. Add drained tin of white beans (Weiße Bohnen). Simmer 10 more min. One pan.
